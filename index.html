The way it works

A row, with
room to work.

TileMe handles placement.
You decide what belongs beside you.

New windows find their place.

Open an app and its window joins after the selected one. Existing windows keep their widths.

Scroll as far as you need.

Hold Control + Option and scroll horizontally on your trackpad to move continuously along the row.

Move without reaching for the mouse.

Go to the previous or next window with your keyboard. The row follows your selection.

Give each window its own width.

Cycle the selected window’s width independently. A wide editor can sit beside a narrow set of notes.

Put things back when you’re done.

Stop the row or quit TileMe normally to restore windows to their original positions.

v0.6.0 preview release notes ↗

  1. Get the app.

    Download TileMe 0.6.0 and open the DMG.

  2. Move it in.

    Drag TileMe into Applications, then eject the disk image.

  3. Open TileMe.

    Launch the installed app from Applications. See the first-launch note below if macOS blocks it.

  4. Give it access.

    Enable TileMe in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

  5. Start your row.

    Press Control + Option + P. Press it again to stop and restore.

Current boundaries

A preview.
A few practical details.

Here’s what to know before you install.

  • Apple Silicon only.Requires macOS 13 or later.
  • One display. One current Space.Switching Spaces stops the row and attempts restoration.
  • Apps still have their own rules.Some apps impose window-size or position restrictions.
  • Quit normally to put things back.Force-quitting cannot restore window positions.
  • Fresh-install compatibility testing is still pending.This is an early preview release.
